Research

Nothing here is called correct until speakers and reviewers have checked it. Every record says who supplied it and how sure we are.

Evidence in the repository

Read live from the project's files. Most records are still unverified leads, and that is stated plainly.

    How we work

    • Every spelling is kept side by side. There is no single "right" one yet.
    • Records start as unverified leads and move up evidence levels only through speaker and reviewer checks.
    • AI helps with chores such as formatting. Its output is never treated as evidence.
    • Everything is public domain (CC0). Every change and deletion is recorded in the open.

        Contribute

        You do not need to be a linguist. Fixing a record means editing a file in the repository, and every tool above updates by itself.

        Speak

        Add words and sentences in Dadi, in any spelling you would use with a friend.

        Check

        Tell us when an entry is wrong or you say it differently. Speakers from every area are needed.

        Be interviewed by an AI

        Use any chatbot with the project's interview prompt, then send the result.

        Lend some AI time

        Spare tokens on your own AI account can do careful, checked work in small chunks.

        Moderate

        Review learning videos and photos so learners only see good material.

        Build

        The code, data and notes are open. A hand-off guide explains how it all fits.

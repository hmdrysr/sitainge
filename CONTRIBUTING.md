# Contributing

**Easiest way:** the chatbot interview in `contribute/README.md`. It produces a ready-to-submit file, and a chatbot that can create GitHub issues can submit it for you with your permission.

You do not need to know YAML, Git or linguistics. Use the issue forms under **Issues > New issue**, or send audio and notes to the project steward.

You can submit: a word, a variant, a grammar observation, a pronunciation, a regional difference, a proverb, idiom, song, story, place name or oral history, a correction, a source, a research question, a possible Rohingya relationship, or an orthographic proposal.

Tell us what you know and how you know it: who said it, where they are from, roughly what age group, and whether you have a recording. Say "not sure" where you are not sure. That is useful evidence.

Your submission is a contribution, not an automatic acceptance. It will be reviewed, and disagreement is recorded rather than erased.

Licence and consent: public contributions are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0, which cannot be undone once published. If you are not sure, choose "Discuss first" and nothing will be published. Recordings that must stay research-only, restricted or private are not uploaded here; contact the project steward about the archive. Speakers are identified by ID (for example `CTG-SPK-00041`), not by name, unless they consent.

Credit: you may be credited by name, by contributor ID, or anonymously.

## Notes
- Different spellings of one name are kept side by side. No spelling is declared the correct one.
- Linguistic classification, political classification, community identity and administrative terminology stay separate (see master prompt section 3). Sources disagree on classification and intelligibility; see decision D-004.
- Needed: how speakers in different localities and generations name the language, and any historical names, collected with consent.

# Audit of the 2019 materials

Materials: (1) "Chittagonian Vocabulary / Cgn-En Translation", Hamid Yasir, September 25, 2019; (2) "Documentation of the Chittagonian Language", Hamid Yasir, September 25, 2019 (alphabet draft).

## What they are
The materials are private, locally sourced community material and a valuable starting point. Under the source hierarchy they are single-source, unrecorded and unlocalized, so they enter as RAW with evidence level `unassessed`.

## Findings
1. **30 starter entries** and **2 sentences**. No speaker, locality, recording or elicitation date is recorded.
2. **Items to verify with speakers** (nothing concluded): *Tébil*, *Sierr*, *Tenk iu*, *Tenks* and *Sori* may be loanwords, native words or code-switches. They need native-speaker verification.
3. **Variants submitted together** (`Badda / By`, `Ken / Kene`, `Ai / Ayunn`) are stored as variants of one record and are not merged away.
4. **UDHR Article 1** is stored as a corpus item; the translator and dialect are unknown. The bracketed line under it in the 2019 file is unexplained, so its provenance is unknown. No morpheme analysis will be made until a speaker reviews the item.
5. **Frequency lists** (about 4,000 English words, with an empty Chittagonian column) are an elicitation frame, not data.
6. **Alphabet draft:** its phonetic notes describe Turkish orthography (examples *kar/kâr*, *gelmek*, *değil*), not Chittagonian. They cannot be used as Chittagonian phonology.
7. **Design conflict:** the draft uses Turkish letters and about 30 accented vowels, while the master prompt requires ordinary Latin keyboards, mobile typing and optional diacritics. See Hamidian Script v0.
8. **Difference in goals:** the 2019 draft says "develop a new writing script and grammar". The master prompt says that the language is documented, not invented. Script design remains legitimate; grammar is documented, not designed.
9. **Method:** the evidence hierarchy replaces "not from libraries": speaker evidence comes first, with published scholarship alongside.

## Established / uncertain / missing
- Established: nothing linguistic.
- Uncertain: every form, gloss and spelling.
- Missing: speakers, localities, recordings, IPA and consent.
- Required fieldwork: elicit the 30 items and 2 sentences from at least two independent speakers in different localities, recorded with consent.

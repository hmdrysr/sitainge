## Claims seen only in encyclopedias (to verify, not accepted)

- Speaker estimates of about 13 to 16 million.
- Intelligibility with Bangla is described differently across sources. **The disagreement is recorded; no choice has been made.**
- Historical use of the Arabic script; Bengali and Latin scripts in use today.
- Preverbal negation, SOV order, contrastive nasalization and a large fricative inventory. All need grammar-level sources.

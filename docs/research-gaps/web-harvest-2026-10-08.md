## Phoneme inventory (source note only; English Wikipedia, as printed)

Consonants: p b t̪ d̪ ʈ ɖ k ɡ; f~ɸ s ʃ x z ɣ; m n ŋ; ɾ~r; l; (w) j.
Vowels: i u e o (ɛ) ɔ æ a. Seven nasalized: ĩ ẽ æ̃ ã ɔ̃ õ ũ. [ɛ] is given as an allophone of /æ/.
The page says Chittagonian is written in Bengali-Assamese script and Latin (Arabic also in the infobox).

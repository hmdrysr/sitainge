# Third owner vocabulary upload (IPA with Latin romanization)

Added on 2026-10-08 from `sitainge_chittagonian_ipa_romanized_large.json` (201 entries, no Bangla script; a scan of U+0980 to U+09FF found none).

## What was added

- 65 new RAW records, CTG-LEX-RAW-04200 to CTG-LEX-RAW-04264, in `lexicon/raw/2026-10-08-ipa-romanized-owner-upload.jsonl`.
- 136 entries were not added as new records because the same gloss and form were already in the repository from the two earlier uploads. Nothing was discarded: the file's content is the same forms.
- The compiler's confidence labels are kept in the record notes only. They are not evidence levels. Every record is `unassessed`, `ai-drafted-unverified` for the IPA, and `research-only`.

## Cautions

- The Latin romanizations are the compiler's, not a speaker's spelling. They are kept as spelling candidates and flagged in `form_note`.
- The file's source label "Ahmed and Khanam 2019" is the same paper as the existing source SRC-IPA-RJOE. No new source record was needed.
- The first block of the file cites ChatgaiyyaBench (CC BY 4.0) as provenance; no Bangla-script forms were taken from it here. Licence compatibility with CC0 is still unconfirmed.
- Each IPA string is unchecked. Some use symbols such as the wedge-like character in "anjam" that may be a conversion error for a vowel.

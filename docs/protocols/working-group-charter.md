# Orthography working group: charter (draft)

Status: draft for discussion, October 2026. Nothing in this charter has been agreed by a group yet. Members, dates and decision rules in square brackets are proposals.

## Purpose

To advise the siṭaiṅge project on how siṭaiṅga is written, using evidence from speakers, and to record decisions and disagreements openly. The group advises on the reference spelling layer (the Hamidian Script work in `docs/hamidian-script/`). It does not overwrite how people write in everyday life: both layers coexist, and old spellings stay valid as variants.

## Members

[Seven to nine members.] Speakers from several upazilas of Chittagong and Cox's Bazar, teachers, writers, and at least one linguist. Members take part as individuals, and the project records their names only with their agreement. The project lead convenes but does not vote.

## Method

1. **Inventory.** Collect the spellings now in use (in each script, and mixed), with who uses them. No judgement at this stage.
2. **Phonemic evidence.** Gather recordings and minimal pairs for each contrast before proposing a symbol.
3. **Candidate conventions.** One or more proposals, each with a stated reason, kept in `docs/hamidian-script/`.
4. **Testing.** Readers and writers try each proposal on words they have not seen. Record error rates and preferences.
5. **Decision.** Taken by [consensus, or a two-thirds majority of members present, with a quorum of five]. Dissent is recorded by name if the member agrees, otherwise without a name.
6. **Versioning.** Each decision gets an identifier and a date in `docs/hamidian-script/orthographic-decision-log.md`. Earlier versions are kept.
7. **Review.** Every [12] months, or sooner on request of [three] members.

## Rules

- Evidence comes first. Popularity is not evidence: likes, stars and majority clicks do not decide a spelling.
- Neither Bangla nor Rohingya is an authority over Chittagonian. No form is "corrected" toward either.
- AI may format and compare spellings on request, but it may not rank spellings or decide the orthography.
- Meetings are recorded only with consent. Minutes are public unless a member asks for an item to be private and the group agrees.
- Members may leave at any time. Their contributions already given remain under the licence they agreed to.

## Conflicts and disagreements

A disagreement is logged with each position and its reasons. The group may adopt more than one convention for different purposes. An unresolved question is recorded as open, not closed by default.

## Open questions for the first meeting

Who else should be invited; how meetings are held for members with limited connectivity; the meeting language; whether decisions need community endorsement beyond the group.

## Disputed material
Whether Chittagonian and Bangla are mutually intelligible: sources disagree. The disagreement is recorded, not resolved (D-004).

## Data in this release
30 RAW lexical records and 3 RAW corpus items. All have evidence level `unassessed` and consent status `permission pending`.

## Known limitations
No record has a speaker, locality, recording or IPA. There is no verified grammar or phonology. Four sources were checked, and none was read in full. The Hamidian Script has no fixed letter mapping. The Editorial Board, Project Steward and reviewers have not yet been appointed. The contributor agreement has not yet been drafted.

## Research gaps
Established: nothing linguistic. Uncertain: every recorded form. Missing: speakers, recordings, regional data and grammar sources. Required fieldwork: recorded elicitation with at least two independent speakers in different localities, with consent. Open questions: the loanword or native status of several items; the scope of Latin spellings already in use; vitality data.

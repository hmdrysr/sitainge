## Known limitations
No speaker, locality, recording or IPA for any record. No verified grammar or phonology. Four sources checked, none read in full. Hamidian Script has no fixed letter mapping. Editorial Board, Project Steward and reviewers not yet appointed. Contributor agreement not yet drafted.

## Research gaps
Established: nothing linguistic. Uncertain: every recorded form. Missing: speakers, recordings, regional data, grammar sources. Required fieldwork: recorded elicitation with at least two independent speakers in different localities, with consent. Open questions: loanword vs native status of several items; scope of Latin spellings already in use; vitality data.
